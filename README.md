# MediaTok iOS

**English** · [Русский](README.ru.md)

**MediaTok** is an enhanced unofficial Telegram client for iOS built on [Telegram iOS](https://github.com/TelegramMessenger/Telegram-iOS) (13.1.1). All extra tools, customization, privacy controls, and MediaTok Lab features live in **Settings → MediaTok**.

MediaTok is not affiliated with Telegram.

## Highlights

- **MediaTok Lab (`#501–#505`):** Stealth Photo Steganography (`.stego`), Voice FX Studio (`.vfx`), AES-256-GCM Burn-After-Reading notes (`.burn`), Cinematic Quote Cards (`.cinematic`), and OSINT X-Ray for EXIF/GPS/links (`.xray`).
- **Discord-style Visual Profile & Sync v2:** 4K looping video banners, Nitro themes, avatar frames & effects, live Steam / Roblox / Twitch / GitHub / music connections, weather wallpapers, Profile Sandbox, and Ed25519-signed sync.
- **Privacy & Security:** Ghost Mode (read/typing/online controls & per-chat exceptions), anti-delete & edit history, screenshot/copy bypass, chat lock with Face ID, secret folder, Safe Mode, and metadata stripping.
- **Media, Audio & Voice:** +15 dB Quiet Voice Booster, timestamped voice transcript search, synced LRC lyrics, Voicemod presets, 4K 60 FPS video export, AR camera masks, call recording & soundboard.
- **Stickers, OCR & Tools:** 10,000-sticker Vault (`/stickers`) with offline Vision OCR & Wi-Fi PC uploader, Sticker Pack ZIP downloader, Sticker Constructor, Manga/Photo OCR Translator, Magic Eraser, 4K Upscale, Face Blur, Document Scanner → PDF + signature, Reverse Image Search, RSS Reader, Spotlight Command Palette.
- **Network & Multi-Account:** Built-in VLESS VPN (`Xray-core`) with Split Tunneling, unlimited accounts with per-account proxy, DC1–DC5 speedtest, DNS-over-HTTPS, and SHA-256 release verification.
- **Mini Apps & Channels:** Mini Apps Power Pack (multi-window, macros, fullscreen/haptics block, platform spoof), Channel Time Machine, Chat Activity Graph, Top Channel Posts, and Fake Live Location.
